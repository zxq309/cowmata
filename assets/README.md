# Asset provenance

Official COWMATA brand/product assets and the conceptual hero were relocated from the recognition repository on 2026-09-07, under the owner's instruction. File SHA-256 and previous paths are recorded in [the migration manifest](../docs/ASSET_MIGRATION.json).

- Brand assets originally came from `https://www.cowmata.com/assets/dist/img/logo_cowmata.svg` and `https://www.cowmata.com/assets/dist/img/logo.png`.
- Product images originally came from `https://www.cowmata.com/assets/dist/img/pro_01.png` and `https://www.cowmata.com/assets/dist/img/pro_02.png`.
- `figures/cowmata-ai-pipeline-hero.png` is conceptual project art originally prepared on 2026-08-18. It is not a real application screenshot or evidence of validated monitoring.
- `figures/cowmata-framework-en.png` and `figures/cowmata-framework-zh.png` preserve the historical 2026-08-18 framework. The current four-repository diagrams are `system-en.svg` and `system-zh.svg`; the historical diagrams do not establish current implementation status.
- Current SVG figures are rebuilt by [build_diagrams.py](../scripts/build_diagrams.py), following [the project design system](../docs/DESIGN_SYSTEM.md).
- `screenshots/system-demo.png` and `screenshots/system-demo-en.png` are Chinese/English screenshots of the bundled illustrative demo, not farm observations.

Company brand and product assets remain proprietary under [NOTICE](../NOTICE).
